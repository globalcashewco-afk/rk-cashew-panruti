Questions

Before you order.

Which grade should I choose?

W210/W240 are larger whole kernels; W320/W400 are smaller whole kernels; SWP, SP, JB and JH are broken/piece categories. Ask on WhatsApp if you need help choosing.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown on this demo are editable. Final quote can vary with quantity, stock and delivery, especially for bulk orders.

Do you deliver across India?

The website is designed for India-wide enquiries. Confirm the final delivery charge and timeline for your pincode before payment.

How do I place an order?

Add the grades and quantities you want, enter your details and send the generated order summary on WhatsApp.

Can regular customers ask for a special price?

Yes, the site provides a dedicated bulk/regular-customer enquiry flow. Final pricing should be confirmed directly with the business.
